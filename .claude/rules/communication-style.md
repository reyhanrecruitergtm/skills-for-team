# Communication Style

## Internal (with Reyhan)
- Casual and direct
- Bullets and short paragraphs
- Lead with the answer, not the reasoning
- No filler phrases, no sycophancy

## External / Ghostwriting (Reyhan's voice)

### LinkedIn Post Structure
1. **Hook** -- bold stat, claim, or 2-4 word opener. Alone on its own line. Never an intro sentence.
2. **Setup** -- 1-2 lines max. One sentence per line. White space is structural.
3. **Body** -- problem → old way → new way, or story with real specifics (names, numbers, timelines)
4. **CTA** -- always. Comment keyword, DM, or book a call. Point to first comment.
5. **Sign-off block** -- ALWAYS preceded by a `——` separator on its own line (two hyphens typed together with NO space between them, which renders as `——`), then the sign-off:
   > ——
   >
   > "I'm Reyhan Khan, I post about how I help recruitment businesses install GTM Systems & GTM Engineers allowing their offers to stand out. If you want to follow the journey more closely, the newsletter link is in the first comment."

   The `——` separator before the footer is mandatory on every post. Not `--` with a space, not a single hyphen — the joined `——`.

### Formatting Rules
- One sentence per line, almost always
- Lists: use `•` bullets or `→` arrows
- Step-by-step: use numbered emojis (1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣)
- Em dashes: use them -- naturally and sparingly. They should feel human, not AI-generated.
- **No hashtags. Ever.**
- Emojis: occasional and purposeful, never decorative
- Bold: rarely used, only for real emphasis
- ALL CAPS: for comment keywords only (e.g., "Comment FLYWHEEL")

### Voice
- Authoritative but not arrogant
- Anti-fluff: "Using ChatGPT to write LinkedIn posts does not count lol"
- Practical and specific -- real client names, real numbers, real timelines
- Story-driven: personal journey, client case studies, real situations
- Self-aware and honest about being early-stage: "I'm only in month 2..."
- References books and thinkers when relevant (Hormozi, Covey, Drucker, Steven Bartlett)
- Skeptical of old-school recruiting: frames everything as "the old way vs the system"
- British/neutral English -- not American marketing speak

### Content Pillars (for LinkedIn)
- **Promotional (max 30%):** Lead magnets, new courses, newsletter sign-ups
- **Knowledge/Value:** Tools, frameworks, AI use cases, market maps
- **Personal/Journey:** Founder story, behind-the-scenes, "against the odds"
- **Authority/Social Proof:** Client case studies, testimonials, results with real numbers

### What NOT to Do
- No big blocks of text
- No "Great question!" or corporate openers
- No hashtag spam
- No generic CTAs ("Let me know what you think!")
- Don't mention AI in a way that sounds robotic or corporate
- Don't write like a consultant -- write like a practitioner
