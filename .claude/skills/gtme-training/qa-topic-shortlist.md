   Our angle: a signal-based BD system that makes deal flow predictable instead of feast/famine.

2. **$800K in year one — when AI runs your cold outreach**

4. **Become the go-to recruiter in your niche in 90 days**

5. **Get clients from LinkedIn without becoming a full-time content creator**

6. **Escape "Chief of Everything": scale without working more hours**

7. **End cold calling: turn LinkedIn into your agency's growth engine**

   Our angle: multichannel sequencing (LinkedIn + email + voice) orchestrated by intent, not one channel.

8. **The 3 AI agents every recruitment agency should run**
   Automating sourcing, BD, and RecOps with AI + no-code at lower cost than hiring. (Deep Singh's keynote theme — our nearest parallel.)
   Our angle: how we actually build them in Claude Code — opinionated stack, live.

9. **Boolean is back: why AI-only sourcing fails and the hybrid that wins**
   AI + Boolean beats AI alone; humans still win on pattern-matching. (Vanessa Raath's most-taught topic.)
   Our angle: the SourcingOS hybrid — signals + Boolean + enrichment in one pipeline.

10. **The recruiter as "AI orchestrator": what to automate, what to never hand to AI**
    The permission structure for adopting AI without cannibalizing your team's value. (Glen Cathey + Josh Bersin's hardest-pushed, most-cited theme.)
    Our angle: the line between machine work and relationship work — and the data (trust gap, abandonment) to back it.
