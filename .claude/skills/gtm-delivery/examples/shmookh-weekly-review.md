# Example — Shmookh Weekly Review Thread (June 2026)

The real exchange this skill is modelled on. Use it to calibrate the **voice** (Rule D) and the **depth** of a good per-account read. Reyhan flags specifics → GTME replies with focus-of-week → Reyhan adjusts ownership/strategy.

---

## Reyhan → Shmookh (the flag)

> Hey bro, my thoughts on your accounts and what needs to be fixed:
>
> **Justin D** — we have no wins and not enough outreach launched. He told me at the start he wants this done for him, and it looks like we're looking for approvals from him for everything multiple times a week and have not gotten enough out.
> **Duncan** — I saw you responded with "in the dataset it was clear what pieces we could not capture through scrape vs what we found through scrape plus Law Society collation." I don't understand what that means or where I can see what he asked for in terms of data vs what we delivered.
> **Patrick How** — 0 campaigns on LinkedIn and only 1 on email. The posts for both Patrick and Justin are too long sometimes and the post-approval process needs to be a lot better. We should have dialed this in — it's been 50 days now.
> **Justin W** — He's complaining about us reaching out to the wrong people. Communication isn't up to the mark on this channel — he still messages me on WhatsApp at least once a week.
>
> Please let me know how you're approaching them.

**What makes this good:** every account leads with the specific gap (no wins / 0 LinkedIn campaigns / 50 days in), names the real friction (approval surface, comms), and ends asking for the GTME's plan.
