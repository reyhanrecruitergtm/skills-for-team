# Experiments Bank

The library Reyhan pulls from in Step 5 to give each GTME 2–3 things to test per account each week. Goal: keep clients feeling progress, keep testing new angles, and push every account toward the full Transformation Standard (Claude + 4 signals + lead magnet + newsletter on top of engine results).

Match experiments to (a) the engine and (b) the gaps surfaced in the review. Don't dump all of them — pick the 2–3 that move *this* account this week.

---

## Cross-cutting (every client, every engine — the transformation layer)

- **Claude Ops Manager install** — set up their Claude for daily ops + productivity (the baseline transformation deliverable).
- **Connect Claude to their tools** — wire MCPs / integrations to their ATS, CRM, email, LinkedIn, sheets so they operate from inside Claude. Prioritise their source-of-truth system first (ATS or CRM).
- **Monthly data-refresh agent** — AI agent that refreshes + re-tags their DB custom fields (cheap model). Huge for any client with a stale database (see Responsum).
- **Lead magnet** — salary guide / market map / niche report, signed off by Claude, gated behind an email capture.
- **Newsletter** — fortnightly/weekly nurture pulling recent candidates, roles, market news, internal wins. Feeds the lead magnet's nurture sequence.
- **VSL + book-a-call funnel** — upgrade the lead-magnet CTA into a short VSL → booked call.
- **Welcome / nurture sequence** — 4–5 email onboarding sequence off the lead magnet, split by engagement.

## OutboundOS experiments

- **Add a signal** toward the ≥4 minimum — hiring, job change, funding (Series A/B), promotion, tech-stack change, leadership move, M&A.
- **New copy angle per signal** — one distinct trigger-tied angle each; rotate hooks.
- **Job Change signal campaign** — segment by C-level / VP / Director and run tailored copy per tier.
- **Channel stack** — layer LinkedIn + email + (where it fits) SMS / ringless voicemail.
- **A/B test** subject lines + first-line personalization; cut losers weekly.
- **ABM micro-campaign** for a handful of dream accounts (hyper-personalised).
- **Deliverability check** — warm-up status, inbox placement; move to HyperTide mailboxes if volume is capped.

## SourcingOS experiments

- **Per-role pipeline maximisation** — for each evergreen role, map every sourcing route and find untapped supply (where are we only catching a slice?).
- **Candidate signals** — tenure 2–3yr (open to move), job change (nurture candidate + BD their old employer), project wins at target firms, promotions (re-tag seniority), open roles to source against.
- **Database re-engagement** — run the existing DB through enrichment, re-score, revive "ready to move" segments.
- **Voice-note / messaging rotation** — refresh angles on the evergreen LinkedIn connection campaign.
- **Director / senior nurture track** — dedicated newsletter + personalised outreach for the high-fee tier (validate market size first).
- **Email enrichment uplift** — Prospeo layer to scale find-rate beyond free tools / limited ATS credits.

## ContentOS experiments

- **Tighten the approval process** — move approvals into Supergrow (not sheets): draft → Reyhan approves → client. Faster throughput, fewer breaks.
- **Post length** — shorter, punchier posts when long ones underperform; one idea per post.
- **Branded imagery** — consistent per-client branding on every graphic.
- **Engagement-rate tracking** — report week-over-week % lift; double down on top formats.
- **Repurpose** — turn a winning post into newsletter + short-form; mine call recordings for content.
- **Cadence lock** — a week of approved posts banked ahead so travel/absence doesn't break the streak.

## Diagnostic questions (Reyhan's coaching pattern)

Use these to pressure-test a GTME's plan when reviewing an account:
- Is this account where it should be on the DeliveryOS timeline for its day-count? If not, why?
- Is ownership crystal clear (primary vs support), or is the client bouncing between people?
- Are we waiting on the client for approvals too often — did they ask for DFY? Reduce the approval surface.
- How many signals are actually live vs the ≥4 standard?
- Does this client have the full transformation (Claude + tools + lead magnet + newsletter), or just the engine?
- What's the client's sentiment — are they messaging Reyhan directly (a comms red flag for that channel)?
- What's the ONE thing that, if launched this week, most moves this account?
