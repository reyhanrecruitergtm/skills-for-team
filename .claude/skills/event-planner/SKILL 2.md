# Event Planner Skill

## Standard pillars (every year)

### 1. Accommodation (villa search)

**Booking nights: 3 nights — Friday + Saturday + Sunday, check out Monday.** Event content runs Fri-Sun but attendees stay Sunday night so the Sunday half-day stays relaxed and people can depart Monday at their own pace.

**Must-haves:**
- 10+ beds (single rooms preferred, max 2 sharing)
- WiFi strong enough for 10 simultaneous laptops + group video calls
- Common area for 10 to work together (long table + space for projector / TV mount)
- Kitchen big enough for catered breakfasts
- Outdoor terrace + pool ideal
- Parking for 1 large van
- Within 5 min drive of Caparica beach

**Search platforms:** Vrbo, Airbnb (filter "luxury villa, 10+ beds, pool"), Plum Guide, local Almada/Setúbal villa rental specialists.
**Booking window:** Lock by end of May for August event.
**Backup:** Always have a second villa on hold in case primary cancels.

### 2. Schedule template (LOCKED 2026 edition)

**Format: 2 nights, 3 days — Fri 21 Aug to Sun 23 Aug.** Attendees check in Friday morning, check out Monday at their own pace. Reyhan + content team arrive Friday morning for villa setup (no Thursday pre-night).

**Friday 21 Aug — Arrive + Learning + Sunset**
- Late morning arrivals at the villa
- Group lunch at the property (welcome + intros)
- Afternoon: 1 learning activity (workshop on the villa terrace)
- Sunset at Caparica beach with food and wine

**Saturday 22 Aug — Work + Mindset + Sights**
- Breakfast at the villa
- Activity 1: work (BD / sourcing / systems sprint at the property)
- Activity 2: mindset (founder reset session at the property)
- Evening: Lisbon sightseeing + Q&A in the streets (walking format, no formal venue)

**Sunday 23 Aug — Reset + 1:1 Roadmaps**
- Morning yoga class
- Future Systems Roadmap activity — 30-min 1:1 with Reyhan per attendee (5 hours back-to-back, builds each attendee's personal 90-day plan)
- Casual goodbye, attendees can stay overnight and depart Monday at their own pace

**Format notes:** This edition is lighter on activity volume than v1 of the playbook — no surf class, no Cabo da Roca, no Cristo Rei. Trade-off is more reflection time and the 1:1 roadmap session, which is the highest-value deliverable per attendee.

### 3. Content team

- Local photographer + videographer (3-day shoot)
- Graphic designer (post-event editing)

**Deliverables per attendee:**
- 1 LinkedIn reel (launching their first BD campaign)
- Multiple post graphics (sized for LinkedIn)
- Group footage shared for RecruiterGTM marketing reuse

**Delivery:** within 5 working days of event end, via Drive folder per attendee.

### 5. Transport

- 15-seater van + English-speaking driver (3 days, Fri-Sun)
- Optional airport pickup from Lisbon LIS (~25 min to Caparica, €30 via Uber if attendees self-arrange)

### 6. Catering

- 2 breakfasts at villa (Sat + Sun)
- 1 BBQ dinner at villa (catered, Sat night)
- 1 dinner at Cascais rooftop (Fri night — book by June for August)
- 1 seafood lunch in Caparica (Sat)
- 1 Sunday lunch
- Drinks at villa: wine, beer, water, coffee, snacks throughout

### 7. RecruiterGTM Welcome Gift Bag

**Every attendee gets a custom gift bag on Day 1.** This is the brand moment — make it feel like a real welcome from Reyhan, not generic event swag.

**Locked items:**
- Custom **RecruiterGTM tote bag** (carries everything else)
- **A5 hardcover notebook** branded RecruiterGTM
- **Pen with logo**
- **Sticker pack** (RecruiterGTM + Claude themed)
- **Custom Lisbon postcard** with a **handwritten welcome note from Reyhan** (this is the moment — every attendee gets a personal note)
- **Reusable water bottle** (logo)
- **Sunscreen + lip balm** (small, practical, Lisbon-summer relevant)
- **Pastel de Nata voucher** — pre-paid at a famous bakery (Manteigaria or Pastéis de Belém), real local touch
- **Schedule card** with all 3 days mapped out

**Optional premium add-ons:**
- Bottle of Porto or local Lisbon wine (per attendee)
- Lisbon-themed art print (commission from a local artist for a unique annual edition)
- RecruiterGTM-branded cap or t-shirt

**Vendor categories to source:**
- Apparel + tote: Printful, local Lisbon screen printer
- Notebooks: Moleskine custom branded, or local Portuguese stationer
- Stickers: StickerMule, Sticker Ninja
- Postcards: local Lisbon designer for the card + handwritten by Reyhan or Xarlish
- Wine: local Lisbon wine shop (better than supermarket)

### 8. Payment + comms

- Stripe checkout for seats
- Account for ~3% processing fee in seat price
- Group WhatsApp pre-event (logistics + intro thread)
- Welcome packet sent 4 weeks before
- Dietary requirements + arrival info collected via Typeform

### 9. Insurance + visa + contingency

- Public liability insurance for the 3 days (some villas require)
- Visa check for non-EU attendees (Portugal Schengen — most US/UK passports don't need one, confirm by passport country)
- Backup villa on hold
- Weather contingency activity (if surf canceled: Setúbal wine tasting, Lisbon walking tour, Sintra day trip)
