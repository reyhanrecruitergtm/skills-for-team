# Command: account-based

Design and execute multi-threaded outreach into a single target account. Used when a company is high-value enough to warrant coordinated, layered outreach rather than single-contact campaigns.

---

## When to Use

- A dream account hasn't responded to standard outreach
- The target company has multiple relevant decision makers
- A client asks "how do we break into [Company]?"
- High-ACV opportunity where a single contact is too risky
- After a referral into a company — build on the warm signal

---

## When NOT to Use

- Volume campaigns (use standard sequence instead)
- Low-ACV targets — ABM requires more time and personalisation than it's worth for small deals
- Companies with fewer than 20 employees — too few stakeholders for multi-threading

---

## Account Selection Criteria

Before building an ABM play, confirm the account passes this filter:

| Criteria | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Revenue potential | High enough to justify 3+ hours of setup |
| Decision maker reachable | At least 2 contacts with LinkedIn profiles |
| Buying signals present | At least 1 Tier 1 or Tier 2 signal active |
| Previous outreach | Single contact tried and no response, OR warm signal via referral |

---

## The 3-Thread Model

For any ABM campaign, identify 3 angles of entry:

### Thread 1 — The Economic Buyer (EB)
The person who signs the budget. Usually: CEO, Founder, MD, COO, VP People.

Goal: Get their attention with a commercial angle. Connect the offer to their growth target or cost problem.

Tone: Peer-to-peer. Brief. No pitch dump.

---

### Thread 2 — The Champion (Internal Influencer)
The person who will advocate internally. Usually: Head of Talent, TA Manager, HR Lead.

Goal: Get them to say "this is relevant to us" and either reply or forward internally.

Tone: Practical. Specific to their job function. Acknowledge they're the expert.

---

### Thread 3 — The Blocker (if identified)
Someone who might resist the solution — or who has tried something similar before.

Goal: Acknowledge their likely objection before it's raised. Convert them from blocker to neutral or supporter.

Tone: Respectful of their expertise. "I know you've probably seen this before."

---

## Multi-Threading Rules

1. **Stagger by 3–5 days** — don't hit all 3 contacts on the same day. Looks coordinated (because it is) but shouldn't look like a blast.

2. **No cross-referencing in opening messages** — don't say "I also reached out to your colleague." Let each thread develop independently first.

3. **If one thread responds positively — pause the others.** Once you have a champion or an EB engaged, you don't need simultaneous threads. Coordinate through the engaged contact.

4. **If two threads respond — acknowledge it naturally.** "I think I've also been in touch with [Name] on your team — happy to loop everyone together."

5. **If no response after 2 full sequences — try one more angle: the personal trigger.** Find something recent about the company (award, news, hiring) and personalise a one-off message.

---

## ABM Sequence Structure

### Thread 1 — Economic Buyer

**Touch 1 (LinkedIn connection note):**
No pitch. Just a hook tied to a business outcome they care about.
```
[Name] — saw [Company] is scaling the [function] team. I work specifically with [company type] on [outcome in their language]. Would be good to connect.
```

**Touch 2 (LinkedIn DM — day 3 after connect):**
One problem. One outcome. One ask.
```
[Name], [observation about their specific situation]. Most [company type] at this stage [common problem]. We've helped [similar company] fix that [result]. Worth 20 minutes?
```

**Touch 3 (Email — day 7):**
Add a proof point. Keep it short.
```
Subject: [Company]'s [function] setup

[Name],

[One-line specific observation from LinkedIn or news].

We recently worked with [similar company] — [result in 1 sentence].

Happy to show you exactly what we did. 20 minutes this week?

[Sign-off]
```

**Touch 4 (Break-up — day 14):**
```
[Name], I won't keep following up after this. If the timing's off, no problem. If [the problem] is something you'd want to look at in Q[X], I'm here.
```

---

### Thread 2 — Champion (TA/HR Lead)

**Touch 1 (LinkedIn connection):**
Acknowledge their expertise first.
```
[Name] — love what [Company]'s been building on the EB/talent side. I work with [function] leads specifically on [specific outcome]. Would be good to connect.
```

**Touch 2 (LinkedIn DM — day 5 after connect):**
Problem framed from their day-to-day perspective.
```
[Name], [specific pain that a TA/HR lead feels — not a CEO pain]. That's exactly the problem we help [function] teams like yours fix. Would any of this be worth a chat?
```

**Touch 3 (Email — day 10):**
Short. Specific. Practical.
```
Subject: One thought on [Company]'s hiring setup

[Name],

[Observation about their open roles or hiring situation].

We've built systems that take the [specific manual task] off TA teams entirely. [One line result].

Would it make sense to connect?
```

---

### Thread 3 — Blocker / Sceptic

**Only use if you know who this person is and why they might block.**

**Touch 1 (LinkedIn connection):**
```
[Name] — you've probably seen a lot of [solution category] pitches. I want to make sure what I'm doing at [Company name/context] actually makes sense before I waste anyone's time. Would be good to connect.
```

**Touch 2 (LinkedIn DM):**
Acknowledge the likely objection directly.
```
[Name], [their likely objection stated plainly]. That's usually because [why it fails in other cases]. What we do differently is [one specific differentiator]. Happy to explain in 15 minutes if that's useful.
```

## Coordinating After Engagement

If a thread responds positively, the next steps depend on who replied:

| Who replied | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| EB | Pause all other threads. Book the meeting. |
| Champion | Nurture them toward an intro to the EB. Ask: "Is [EB name] someone I should loop in?" |
| Blocker | Address their concern, then ask for the right person to speak to. |
| Two contacts simultaneously | Acknowledge it naturally, suggest a joint call. |
