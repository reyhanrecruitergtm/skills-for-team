# Command: performance-review

Full campaign debrief. Goes deeper than campaign-health-check — this is the monthly or end-of-campaign analysis that feeds directly into the next campaign's strategy.

---

## When to Use

- End of month for retainer clients (Shmookh delivers this to clients)
- After a campaign runs its full sequence (all contacts finished)
- When deciding whether to continue, pause, or pivot a campaign
- Quarterly business review with Reyhan

---

## Inputs Required

1. **Full campaign stats** — total contacted, connection rate, reply rate, interested, meetings booked, closed
2. **Campaign duration** — start date to end date
3. **ICP targeted** — who we went after
4. **Sequence used** — which playbook/touches
5. **Any context** — what changed during the campaign, unusual events

---

## Review Structure

### Section 1: Campaign Summary

One paragraph. What was this campaign, who did it target, and what was the goal?

---

### Section 2: Performance Scorecard

| Metric | Target | Actual | vs Target |
|--------|--------|--------|-----------|
| Contacts reached | X | X | +/-X% |
| Connection rate (LI) | 35-50% | X% | +/-X% |
| Reply rate | 15-25% (LI) / 3-8% (email) | X% | +/-X% |
| Interested rate | 25-40% of replies | X% | +/-X% |
| Meetings booked | X/week | X total | +/-X% |
| Show rate | >70% | X% | +/-X% |
| Pipeline generated | £/$ X | £/$ X | +/-X% |
| Revenue closed | £/$ X | £/$ X | +/-X% |

---

### Section 3: What Worked

3-5 specific things that drove results. Be precise — not "the copy was good" but "Touch 2's leadership change angle generated 60% of all interested replies."

Format:
```
✅ [What worked]
   Why: [Specific reason]
   Evidence: [Data point]
```

---

### Section 4: What Didn't Work

3-5 specific failures or underperformances. Same precision required.

Format:
```
❌ [What didn't work]
   Why: [Hypothesis]
   Evidence: [Data point]
   Fix for next time: [Specific change]
```

---

### Section 5: ICP Learnings

- Which company profiles converted best?
- Which titles responded best?
- Which signals produced the warmest leads?
- Any segments to remove from future targeting?

---

### Section 6: Copy Learnings

- Which touch had the highest reply rate?
- Which opener angle resonated most?
- Which subject line won?
- Any phrases that consistently triggered positive or negative responses?

---

### Section 7: Forward Recommendations

3 concrete changes for the next campaign. Each must be actionable and specific.

Format:
```
1. [Change] — because [evidence from this campaign]
2. [Change] — because [evidence from this campaign]
3. [Change] — because [evidence from this campaign]
```

---

### Section 8: Client-Facing Summary (for retainer clients)

A short, non-technical paragraph Shmookh can send directly to the client. No jargon. Focuses on outcomes and next steps.
