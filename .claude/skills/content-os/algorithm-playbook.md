## 1. The algorithm now: 360Brew (one unified model)

- One scoring system across all formats — format hacks matter less than value + fit.
- **Profile-content alignment matters**: the algorithm classifies who you are from your profile and rewards posts that match. Reyhan's profile must stay tightly framed on Claude/AI for recruitment agencies — posts inside that lane get distribution, off-lane posts get throttled.
- **Niche content is rewarded**: the system learns your topics and matches you to the right readers. Niche winners beat broad content now. Recruitment-GTM specificity is an advantage, not a limitation.
- Content is judged by meaning and audience, not raw engagement alone. Low-quality engagement (pods, "Great post!") is actively discounted.
- Posts can resurface days later if engagement stays strong — don't judge a post dead at hour 1.
- Hashtags are dead: >3 hashtags = 70% WORSE. (Confirms locked rule: no hashtags, ever.)
- Median impressions are down platform-wide (-66% from 2023 peak). Falling reach is the baseline, not a personal failure. Top content wins 149x vs average — quality gap is everything.

## 2. What to post (topics + formats)

- **Educate, whatever the niche.** Educational content dominates outliers. Career-development angles have a 3x higher outlier probability than any other category — frame posts as "this helps a recruitment agency owner get better at their job."
- **AI/Technology is the ONLY rising category.** Reyhan's exact lane (Claude for recruitment). Lean in hard.
- **Reach by format:** carousels (>5 slides) 4.1x vs text · infographics 3.4x and most likely to go viral · quotes/personal pics lower but build trust. Video = low reach but best for demos + trust (only if Reyhan is IN it).
- **Winning infographic anatomy:** clear large title, problem-focused, bright contrasting colors (red/green misconception-vs-reality works), so actionable it could be printed. Adapt proven viral layouts to the recruitment-GTM niche — niche educational infographics are underserved and stand out.
- **Winning carousels tell stories** with pictures + contrast — not listicles. The title slide is everything: big clear title playing on a relatable fear, scroll-stopping image.
- **Images:** portrait 4:5 (1200x1500px) performs 86% better than landscape; key info centered. Educational, surprising, or authority-projecting (stage, news) images win. Avoid excessive selfies.
- **Video:** horizontal beat vertical by 36% (readable text hooks; vertical feels off at work). Product demos and Reyhan-on-camera build trust; feel-good reposts don't build personal brand.
- Balance educational posts with personal ones (reflections, goals, predictions) — trust and depth.

## 3. How to write (all consistent with locked voice rules)

- **Length: 1,250–3,000 characters** performs 31% better than short. Depth + authenticity beat corporate polish.
- **14+ short paragraphs**: under 7 paragraphs performed 71% worse. (Matches locked one-sentence-per-line style.)
- **Grade 5–7 reading level**: words averaging >5 letters = 39% worse.
- **Conversational, sounds-like-you writing wins 2026**: signature phrases, same energy as on video, personal stories teaching business lessons. (Matches Reyhan's voice rules exactly.)
- **Hooks that produce outliers:** proof of work · proof of authority · unexpected angle · personal story.
- **Provocative statements beat questions**: "Most people get this wrong about X" > "What do you think about X?" Generic engagement-bait questions now perform WORSE. Only ask genuine, specific questions (either/or debates, real scenario asks).
- **Emojis: +220% in the report** — BUT locked rule wins: occasional and purposeful only, in CTAs and to mark key points, never decorative, never AI-typical ones (🚀✨💡).

## 4. Links — conventional wisdom is DEAD

- Posts with 1–3 external links: **43% HIGHER reach**. LinkedIn confirmed adding a link after publishing doesn't affect reach either.
- Implication for the locked sign-off ("newsletter link in the first comment"): first-comment linking is no longer required for reach. Worth A/B testing the newsletter link directly in the post body vs first comment. Do NOT change the sign-off without Reyhan's approval — flag the test, don't just switch.

## 5. When to post

- **Sunday is the best day** (1,531 median impressions) then Saturday (1,368) — less corporate competition, more leisure browsing. No clearly bad weekday.
- Current calendar (Mon/Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat) has no Sunday slot — Sunday is the open opportunity for the most in-depth educational piece of the week.
- Best hours: **11AM–1PM GMT** (~1,900 median). Avoid 4PM–1AM GMT (~500–750).
- Day-type pairing: Thu/Sun = educational deep guides · Fri/Sat = personal stories, feel-good.

## 6. Engagement strategy (off-post work)

- Top 1% creators reply to comments 741% more (286/wk vs 34). Comment volume correlates 0.545 with follower growth.
- 10–20 thoughtful comments daily on niche creators' posts; personal-experience comments, never "Great post!".
- **Engagement from execs and large accounts is a reach multiplier** — a CEO/CMO commenting signals the algorithm to push the post to that whole professional segment. Build relationships with recruitment-industry authority figures (podcast hosts list in `reference_recruitment_podcast_hosts.md`) and help them win first.
- Reply fast to comments on own posts — sustained engagement can resurface the post days later.

## 7. Lead magnets

- **Custom GPTs are the top-performing lead magnet for small accounts right now** (e.g. "Outreach GPT") — it does the work FOR the ICP. Playbooks/one-pagers still work but underperform GPTs.
- Winning funnel: genuinely valuable asset → hook that describes the value → "comment KEYWORD" → landing page that captures email. (Matches Reyhan's keyword CTA system; keywords stay locked per SKILL.md section H.)
- For RecruiterGTM: a "Recruitment GTM GPT" / Claude-based tool giveaway fits the AI-implementer positioning perfectly.

## 8. Outlier system (how to improve week over week)

- Outlier = post that beats YOUR average by 4-5x (use likes as proxy; if average is 10, a 50-like post is an outlier).
- Formula: **Outlier = Topic × Hook × Visual.** Find winners in the niche, adapt (never copy) topic/hook/visual to Reyhan's story.
- Keep a swipe file of niche outliers. Study Reyhan's own top 20% monthly — what made them different. Expect the bottom 80% of posts to earn only ~10% of impressions; that's normal, but learn from every winner.
