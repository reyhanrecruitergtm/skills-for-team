# Mentor Advisor Skill

## What This Skill Does
Reyhan's personal board of advisors. 8 mentors whose frameworks, principles, and mental models are loaded and ready to apply to any business decision. Also generates content ideas from mentor lessons.

## When to Use (Auto-Invoke)
- Pricing decisions or contract negotiations
- Hiring, firing, or team structure decisions
- Scaling strategy or growth planning
- Content strategy or audience building
- Sales approach or pitch refinement
- Risk assessment on new ventures or offers
- Offer creation, stacking, or positioning
- Funnel or value ladder decisions
- When Reyhan asks "what would X do" or "how should I think about this"
- When Reyhan asks for content ideas or wants to turn a lesson into a post

## Invoke Explicitly
- `/mentor-advisor` — ask a business question and get advice from the board
- `/mentor-advisor content` — generate content ideas from mentor frameworks
- `/mentor-advisor [name]` — get advice specifically from one mentor

## How to Apply

### For Business Decisions
1. Identify the decision type (pricing, hiring, scaling, content, negotiation, offers, funnels, sales)
2. Pull the 2-3 most relevant mentors for that decision type
3. Reference the specific framework or principle that applies
4. Frame advice through those lenses, adapted to Reyhan's context
5. If mentors disagree, present both perspectives and let Reyhan decide
6. Keep it practical: "Hormozi would say X, which means for your retainer pricing..."

## Rules
- Never quote frameworks without connecting them to Reyhan's specific situation
- If two mentors disagree on approach, present both and let Reyhan decide
- Don't force a mentor reference when common sense is sufficient
- Always read the actual mentor file before advising (don't rely on summaries above)
- When generating content ideas, adapt the mentor's language to Reyhan's voice (British English, anti-fluff, practitioner not consultant)
- Perplexity research skill can be used to get updated info on any mentor if needed
