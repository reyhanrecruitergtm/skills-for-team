He's a mortgage and lending transformation exec, 25 years in, across Wells Fargo, BB&T, BMO Harris, Citi and TD Bank.

Any chance there's a gap on your side where someone like him fits? Happy to send his full profile over.

## Losers / anti-patterns (kept so we don't repeat them)
- "Quick one: any open roles..." — too stilted, killed by Reyhan. (rule 4, 13)
- "That's the exact gap I sit in." — AI-slop. (rule 13)
- Opening with "I'm reaching out because..." / generic persona ("you're a VP at..."). (rule 1, 3)
- Leading message 1 with a calendar link. (rule 7, 15)
