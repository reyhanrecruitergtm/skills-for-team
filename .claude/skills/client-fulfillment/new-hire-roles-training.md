# New Hire Roles & 30-Day Training Plan

Two roles to bring the team to growth-ready, plus a ramp that also levels up Daniyal + Shmookh. Hiring philosophy (James Caan): **attitude > aptitude.** Clay, copy, and GTM are teachable in weeks; hunger and ownership are not. Hire hungry; train the skills.

---

## Role A — Community Manager + GTM Assistant

**Why:** takes community off Daniyal immediately (Neil Patel — "doing too many things kills all"), and adds cross-account hands so GTMEs spend their time on the 30% custom thinking, not the 70% repeatable build.

**Owns:**
- All Day-30+ community support: Skool inbox daily, weekly Q&A logistics, member check-ins, at-risk member outreach.
- Cross-account assist: list-building, enrichment runs (Clay + Claude OutboundOS skill), CSV hygiene (first/last name split, LinkedIn URL verification), DeliveryOS data entry.
- Monday QA ritual: runs the Definition-of-Done scoring for every active client, flags reds in #ops.

**Does NOT own:** client strategy, copy angles, weekly client calls (those stay with the GTME).

**Success by Day 30:** community fully off Daniyal · QA ritual running every Monday · can stand up a signal table from a [T] recipe unsupervised.

---

## Role B — GTME-in-Training → GTME

**Why:** account capacity is the growth bottleneck. This is the hire that lets you take the May/June retainers and get Reyhan out of his 3 stopgap accounts.

**Ramp:**
- **Day 0–30:** shadow Shmookh + Daniyal on live accounts. Own no accounts. Execute [T] tasks (signals, lead magnets, list builds) under supervision.
- **Day 31–60:** carry 1–2 accounts (ideally Reyhan's stopgap accounts as they reach steady state — lowest handover cost). Run the weekly client call with a GTME on the line.
- **Day 61–90:** full GTME at 3–4 account ceiling. Reyhan fully out of operator mode.

**Success by Day 60:** runs a client end-to-end through a phase · client call solo · positive-reply tracking clean in DeliveryOS.

## 30-Day Training Curriculum (new hires + refresher for Daniyal/Shmookh)

Mix of internal sessions, the skill library, and paid external sessions (e.g. Eric Nowoslawski). **Time the paid sessions after the template library v1 exists** — bring experts in to optimise a system, not design one from scratch. Sharper ROI.

### Week 1 — Foundations
- The 90-day fulfillment SOP end-to-end (`sop-retainer-onboarding.md`) — read + walk a live client.
- The data flow: Clay → DeliveryOS → Instantly/HeyReach. Where quality leaks and how the SOP prevents it.
- Tool fluency: Clay tables, Apollo, Apify, Prospeo, exa, Instantly, HeyReach.
- The 10-signal library — what each signal is and when to use it.

### Week 2 — Copywriting (internal + skill-led)
- The 2-copy framework (no-signal + AI line / signal-matched + AI line).
- Voice rules: humanizer skill, no AI tells, no "ship" language, em-dash discipline.
- The lead-magnet angle: "evolution of recruitment in {niche}" — write 2 practice magnets.
- 1st- and 2nd-connection nurture angles.

### Week 3 — Sales & GTM strategy
- ICP definition + TAM build (`build-tam` skill) — the [C] thinking that differentiates a client.
- Signal selection per niche — which signals fit which client.
- Reading metrics: open/reply/positive-reply, when to kill a campaign, A/B discipline.
- **External paid session** (Eric Nowoslawski or similar) on signal sophistication + deliverability.

### Week 4 — Live execution + assessment
- Stand up a real (or sandbox) client through Phase 1 against the Definition of Done.
- Run a mock weekly client call.
- QA pass: score their build against the SOP checklist. Gaps = the next learning loop.

## What makes someone "5-capable" vs "4"

A GTME holds 5 (not 4) when they: lean hard on [T] templates (low custom time per client), keep DeliveryOS clean so QA is fast, and don't let two clients sit in Phase-1 build at once. Capability = leverage on the system, not raw hours. See `capacity-model.md`.
