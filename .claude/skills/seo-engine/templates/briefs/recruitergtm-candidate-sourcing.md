## FAQ Section

1. Q: What is candidate sourcing in simple terms?
   A: Proactively finding and contacting potential candidates who haven't applied yet — the top of the recruiting funnel, before screening.
2. Q: How is sourcing different from recruiting?
   A: Sourcing starts conversations with passive candidates; recruiting screens and places responders. Different skills, tools, and metrics.
3. Q: What tools automate candidate sourcing?
   A: A find layer (Boolean/saved search), enrichment (Apollo + Apify), an AI layer for personalised outreach (Claude), and a sender (Instantly + HeyReach).
4. Q: How many candidate conversations should a sourcing system generate?
   A: A well-built agency sourcing engine targets 30-50 qualified candidate conversations per month, per pipeline.
