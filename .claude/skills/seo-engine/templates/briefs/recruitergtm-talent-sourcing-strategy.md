## FAQ Section

1. Q: What is a talent sourcing strategy?
   A: A documented plan for how an agency finds and engages candidates before they apply — who to target, which channels, what triggers outreach, and how often, repeatable across roles.
2. Q: How is a sourcing strategy different from a recruiting strategy?
   A: A sourcing strategy covers finding and engaging passive candidates (top of funnel); a recruiting strategy covers screening and closing the ones who respond.
3. Q: What channels should a talent sourcing strategy include?
   A: It depends on the niche — LinkedIn and Boolean search for most desks, plus Meta/Instagram for blue-collar and trade niches where the talent isn't on LinkedIn.
4. Q: How often should a recruitment agency review its sourcing strategy?
   A: Review channel performance monthly and the full strategy (ICP, signals, cadence) quarterly, or immediately after launching in a new niche.
