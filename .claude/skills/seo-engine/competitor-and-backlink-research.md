## Part 1 — Competitor Analysis

### The core finding: two separate competitive fronts

RecruiterGTM competes on two fronts that need different strategies. Conflating them is the classic mistake.

- **Business competitors** (who we fight for *clients*) — barely do SEO. Sparse field.
- **Search competitors** (who we fight for *keywords*) — tools and review sites, not coaches. Strong but generic.

**Read:**
- **No named competitor has a meaningful non-branded search footprint.** The entire named field does < 75 organic visits/month each. The niche is an SEO vacuum.

### Tactic worth stealing — client-name case pages
The Agency Blueprint ranks individual case-study pages for *client names* (adrian fowler, reyna martinez, lara gebka…). When a prospect Googles that client, the coach's success story appears. Zero competition on a person's name. **Action:** build one `/case-study/[client-name]` page per willing client (we already have Patrick Schildmann). Free ranking, social proof, and it interlinks into the pillars.

## Part 2 — Backlink Potential

DR 0.1 means backlinks are the gating constraint on ranking anything. The good news: RecruiterGTM has warm relationships with high-DR, niche-relevant domains that most DR-0 sites could never access. These are the fastest path to DR ~20.

### Relationship assets (live DR)

| Relationship | Domain | DR | Why it matters | Link play |
|-------------|--------|---:|----------------|-----------|
| Clay | clay.com | 79 | Enormous authority + we're a partner/affiliate | Partner/case-study page, "built with Clay" story, expert directory listing (already listed). One dofollow here moves our DR. |
| Instantly | instantly.ai | 79 | Enormous authority + affiliate | Customer story / case study / integration or partner page |
| HeyReach | heyreach.io | 71 | High authority + affiliate | Case study, partner page, co-marketing content |
| Mark Whitby | recruitmentcoach.com | 30 | Niche-perfect (trains 10,000+ recruiters), podcast host | Guest on The Resilient Recruiter → show-notes dofollow link; guest article |
| Benjamin Mena | eliterecruiterpodcast.com | 27 | Niche-perfect + already our strongest proven lead source | Repeat podcast guest → episode-page link; co-created content; newsletter feature |

Tool partners (Clay/Instantly/HeyReach) are the highest *DR* lift; the recruitment podcasters (Mena/Whitby) are the highest *relevance* and audience-quality lift. Pursue both — Google weighs relevance and authority together.

### The LinkedIn factor
Reyhan's LinkedIn presence is a first-class SEO asset even though the links are nofollow:
- **Distribution → earned links.** Every blog post announced on LinkedIn (link in first comment) reaches thousands; a fraction of viewers with their own sites/newsletters reference and link to it. LinkedIn is the top of the link-earning funnel.
- **AI citation + brand signal.** Consistent, on-message LinkedIn content teaches AI engines the RecruiterGTM entity (rule C11) and drives branded search, both of which lift citation odds.
- **Repurposing loop.** Each blog → LinkedIn post/article; each strong LinkedIn post → blog seed. The blog and LinkedIn feed each other.
- **The rule:** every published blog post ships with a LinkedIn distribution asset in the same pass (already in the weekly rhythm, Phase 6). On-page perfection alone doesn't earn citations — 84-93% of AI citation weight is third-party (Aleyda Solis).

### Prioritised backlink action plan (first 90 days)

1. **Cash in the tool-partner links (highest DR, fastest).** Ask Clay, Instantly, HeyReach for a case study / partner-page / customer-story link to a relevant page (not just the homepage). We already have the relationships and affiliate status. Target: 3 dofollow links from DR 71-79 domains. This alone should move DR from ~0 into double digits.
2. **Book the two recruitment podcasts.** Repeat guest on Benjamin Mena + guest on Mark Whitby's Resilient Recruiter → dofollow show-notes links from DR 27-30 niche sites + high-quality referral audience.
3. **Launch one original-data asset** ("State of Recruitment Agency Outbound 2026" from the 650 audits / 50+ deployments) → the citable link magnet for cold outreach to recruitment blogs and journalists.
4. **Systematise HARO-style responses** (Help a B2B Writer, Featured, Qwoted) — Reyhan answers recruitment/AI/GTM queries weekly.
5. **Client-name case pages + client cross-links** — one per willing client; ask for a link back as part of delivery.
6. **LinkedIn → first-comment link on every post**, every time.

**What we never do:** bought links, PBNs, directory spam, exact-match-anchor schemes. Clean and relevant beats fast and dirty at DR 0.1, and Google penalises the dirty version.

**Reality check:** even with these relationships, DR moves over months, not days. Realistic: DR ~15-25 and first cluster rankings within 90 days *if* the partner links land early.
