# OutboundOS-RGTM — Copywriting Framework

The reusable cold copy framework for pitching the RecruiterGTM community, built from **Eric Nowoslawski's template** (`My Drive/RecruiterGTM/Offer & Marketing/Copywriting/Copywriting Template.jpg`) + **Reyhan's proven "Dan" DM**. One spine, 2 variations (V2 + V3), swappable across 5 pillars. Every message runs `copy-engine` best-of-N + `humanizer` before it sends, and is written in **Reyhan's voice per `content-os` Sections A–M** — the taste rules and banned words apply to outbound too: plain practitioner English, reads like a text from a smart friend, **no "lane," no praise of the prospect, no grand claims** ("the only world," "all we do"). These are the control/starting drafts, not the final.

## The proven anchors
- **Reyhan's "Dan" DM (got replies):** `Hey Dan, thanks for connecting.` → `What's a bigger pain point rn, client acquisition or candidate sourcing?` → `Happy to share my top-performing Playbooks and maybe do a brainstorm call if you're open to it.` → `Best, Reyhan`
- **Eric's template spine:** subject (hyper-personal) → not-automated opener → priority question (relevance+offer) → reason + specific proof → soft CTA → PS tie-in.

## The spine (5 beats — same every pillar)
1. **Human opener.** LI: `Hey {{first}}, thanks for connecting.` · Email: `Subject: {{2–4 word, lowercase}}` + `{{first}} - wasn't sure whether to send this to you or {{colleague/partner}}.`
2. **The priority question** (binary, low-friction, pillar-specific pain): `What's the bigger headache right now, {{pain A}} or {{pain B}}?`
3. **Reason + specific proof** (why asking + one real number — verified only): `Only asking because {{their signal}}, and we just {{specific pillar result}}.`
4. **The give** (value-first asset, no call yet): `Happy to send {{the Playbook/skill/teardown for that pillar}}.`
5. **Soft CTC** (a question, not a calendar link): `Worth a look?` / `Open to it?` / `Lmk if that's of interest.` (Email adds Eric's self-aware **PS**.)

## Rules baked in (copy-engine)
One idea per message · signal-led line 1 · binary yes/no ask · CTC not CTA (msg 1 = interest check, call ask comes later) · real proof only (no "200 companies") · em dashes default 0 · banned phrases barred ("quick one", "no pressure either way", "seat", etc.) · 50–90 words email · one sentence per line · British/neutral English · score 85+ before send.
**Proof = verified numbers only.** Use 50+ OutboundOS deployments · 200+ GTME placements (98% still in role at 18 mo) · 650+ agencies audited. PENDING verification (do NOT use until Reyhan confirms): "70+ agencies," "€38,500," "2M impressions."

## The 3 variations (angles — each works across all pillars)

### Variation 1 — The Priority Question (warm / 1st-degree — highest reply)
Reyhan's Dan style. Lead with the binary pain question, give the Playbook, soft optional CTA. Best on LinkedIn 1st-degree + warm email.
> Hey {{first}}, thanks for connecting.
> What's eating more of your week right now, the back-office admin or actually getting in front of clients?
> Reason I ask: I build Claude setups that run the admin side of a recruiter's desk, sourcing, list-building, outreach drafts, so the founder gets the selling time back.
> Happy to send you the exact setup one of our recruiters uses, no call needed.
> Lmk if that's of interest.
> Best, Reyhan

### Variation 2 — The Build / Feedback angle (door-opener, doubles as product research)
Not a pitch — ask their expertise on what we're building. Disarming, flattering, high reply. Reyhan flagged this as the strongest.
> Hey {{first}}, thanks for connecting.
> We're building something for recruitment founders: a Claude "ops manager" that runs sourcing, outreach and your ATS from one place.
> I'd rather get it right with people who actually run desks than guess, so I'm showing it to a few founders in {{niche}} before we lock it.
> Would you take a look and tell me where it'd break for you? Early access if it's useful, and honestly I just want the feedback.
> Open to it?
> Best, Reyhan

### Variation 3 — The Proof / Teardown angle (cold email — Tier A)
Eric's show-don't-tell + self-aware PS. Lead with "I made you a thing." Highest-effort, highest-standout.
> Subject: your {{niche}} sourcing
> {{first}} - made you something before reaching out.
> I mapped {{company}}'s setup against where a Claude sourcing engine would cut your time-to-shortlist (rough, 3-min Loom).
> Not selling anything in it. If it's useful you keep the teardown, if not, no harm.
> Want me to send it over?
> PS - built these for {{X — verified}} agencies now, yours stood out because {{specific reason}}.

Rotate the 3-Offers frame across the follow-up sequence (save time → make money → save money). Log reply rates back to `swipe-file.md`; promote/kill on numbers.

## Variable normalization (run before copy)
- **`{{Company}}` — clean spoken brand name.** Drop legal suffixes (LLC, Ltd, Inc, Corp, Co) and leading "The"; drop generic trailing org words (Associates, Group, Partners, Holdings) when the name still stands; keep distinctive descriptors (Talent, Search, Capital) only if they ARE the brand. Domains → real brand (drop extension/hyphens, expand obvious shortenings). Examples: `Altium Associates → Altium` · `RML Specialty Group, LLC → RML` · `Garvey Executive Search, Inc. → Garvey` · `The Bandish Group, LLC → Bandish` · `cyberaptrecruitment.com → CyberApt` · `Metafora Search → Metafora`. Keep multi-word brands that read as one (`Silicon Valley Human Capital`).
- **`{{niche}}` — short conversational specialism** that slots into "a {{niche}} desk" / "for {{niche}}". Lowercase, 2–4 words, specific. Examples: `life sciences exec search` · `fintech recruitment` · `GTM & RevOps recruiting` · `energy & commodities search` · `healthcare staffing`. Inferred from name/domain/site; if genuinely unclear, use `recruitment` (never guess a wrong corner).

## The `{{icebreaker}}` — line 1 (LOCKED question, Reyhan 2026-08-13)
Line 1 of every message is ONE fixed question; only `{{niche}}` changes per lead. It's the simplest question you can ask a recruiter, and it's specific to their corner. More question variations get added later; for now, this one everywhere.

**The question (verbatim):**
> For {{niche}}, is the harder part surfacing the right candidates or getting the passive ones to engage?

The message then bridges into the build (V2/V3 body): *"Asking because I'm building {{pillar build}} for {{niche}} desks, and I want the {{niche}} part right."* → the ask.

### The cron's only per-lead job: produce a clean `{{niche}}` (or SKIP)
```
You are filling {{niche}} for line 1 of a cold message to a recruitment agency founder.

INPUTS: Company name · Domain · LinkedIn URL · Website summary · LinkedIn headline/about

STEP 1 — NICHE CHECK: confirm this is a recruitment / staffing / executive search / talent
agency. If it is anything else (in-house TA team, job board, RPO-only, software, consulting,
non-recruitment), output exactly: SKIP

STEP 2 — {{niche}}: write their specialism as a short, conversational, lowercase phrase
(2 to 4 words) that reads naturally right after "For ". Only what the inputs actually show.
Examples: energy exec search · marketing and comms search · medical affairs and MSL roles ·
real estate and PE finance search · legal search from associate to partner · aviation search.
If the corner is genuinely unclear, use: executive search.

OUTPUT the finished line, nothing else:
For {{niche}}, is the harder part surfacing the right candidates or getting the passive ones to engage?
```
