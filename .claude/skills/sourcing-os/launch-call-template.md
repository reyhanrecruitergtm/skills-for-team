# SourcingOS Launch Call — Question Template

Use on every SourcingOS launch/kickoff call. SourcingOS sources **candidates** for the client's roles (mirror of OutboundOS, which targets the client's *buyers*). Capture every section, then it feeds the candidate ICP + market map + signal config.

---

## 1. Client & Desk Overview
- Agency name + who's on the recruiting desk (and who works which patch)
- Niche / vertical they recruit in
- Service model: contingency / retained / RPO / embedded
- Who are their **clients** (the companies they place candidates INTO) — sector, size
- What does a win in the next 90 days look like? (candidate conversations / submissions / placements)

## 2. The Roles You Fill (live + evergreen)
- Your 3–5 most common / recurring roles? (these become the evergreen pipelines)
- Any live / priority briefs to source for **first**?
- Per role: permanent vs contract, typical salary/day-rate band, level
- How many of each do you fill per month / quarter?

## 3. Candidate ICP (per role)
- Job titles candidates currently hold (+ variations)
- Seniority / years of experience bands
- Must-have skills, qualifications, certifications, software
- Nice-to-haves
- Location(s) / region(s) · remote / onsite / hybrid
- Salary expectations
- Right-to-work / visa / sponsorship constraints
- Languages

## 4. Where to Source From (talent pools)
- Which companies do your **best candidates come from**? (feeder firms / competitors / target employers)
- "Dream" companies whose people you'd love to approach
- **Off-limits:** current clients you can't poach from, existing placements, do-not-contact
- Anyone already in your pipeline/ATS we should dedupe against

## 5. Boolean / Filters
- Title keywords (include) · Skill keywords (include)
- Exclusion keywords (wrong seniority, wrong discipline, agencies, etc.)

## 6. When to Reach Out — Candidate Signals (tick the ones that matter)
- [ ] Tenure 2–3 years in role (open to a move)
- [ ] Recent promotion
- [ ] Open-to-work / actively looking
- [ ] Employer layoffs / restructure / lost a contract
- [ ] Employer won funding / a big project (hiring + churn)
- [ ] Active / posting on LinkedIn
- [ ] Backfill or re-posted role signal

## 7. Volume + Geography
- Target candidate conversations / month (SourcingOS benchmark: **30–50**)
- Regions to cover + priority order
- **Recruiter ↔ region mapping** (who works which patch)

## 8. Outreach Preferences
- Channels: LinkedIn / email / WhatsApp / call
- **Whose profile do messages send from?** (each recruiter's own, or a brand profile)
- Tone / voice · candidate value prop to lead with
- Messaging to avoid / compliance (e.g. never approach a current client's staff)
- Re-engagement: how to handle candidates who went quiet (build into the sequence)

## 9. ATS, Data & Handover
- ATS in use (Bullhorn, Vincere, JobAdder, RecruiterFlow…)
- Existing candidate database to mine / re-engage?
- Do-not-contact list
- How interested replies get handed back: status logic + source attribution (Contacted → Replied → Screening → Submitted)
- Weekly reporting cadence + Slack channel

## 10. Notes / Constraints
- Regulatory, salary confidentiality, exclusivity, language, sensitivities, anything else
