## How to Apply

### For Business Decisions
1. Identify the decision type (pricing, hiring, scaling, content, negotiation, offers, funnels, sales)
2. Pull the 2-3 most relevant mentors for that decision type
3. Reference the specific framework or principle that applies
4. Frame advice through those lenses, adapted to Reyhan's context
5. If mentors disagree, present both perspectives and let Reyhan decide
6. Keep it practical: "Hormozi would say X, which means for your retainer pricing..."
