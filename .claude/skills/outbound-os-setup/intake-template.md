## 3. ICP Keywords

Used to filter companies in Clay by their company description.

- **Inclusion keywords** (company description should mention these — use AND/OR logic):
  - Example: "hiring", "scaling", "rapid growth", "Series B", "backed by"
- **Exclusion keywords** (filter out companies with these):
  - Example: "NHS", "public sector", "charity", "staffing agency", "recruitment"
