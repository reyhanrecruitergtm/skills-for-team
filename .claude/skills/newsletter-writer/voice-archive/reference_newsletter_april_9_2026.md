1. Connectors - your tools talking to each other
Claude Cowork connects directly to Apollo, Clay, Apify, your CRM, Gmail, LinkedIn. It pulls live data from these tools and acts on it. No copy-pasting between tabs. No CSV exports. You talk to Claude and it talks to your stack.
One recruiter on the call asked me to build a candidate list for a Senior MEP Engineer in Manchester. Claude pulled 20 qualified candidates with names, titles, companies and LinkedIn URLs in under 2 minutes. From scratch.

2. Skills - repeatable workflows you build once and run forever
A Skill is a saved workflow. You build it once and Claude runs it every time you ask. Think of it like training an employee to do your job for you, except this employee never forgets the process, never cuts corners, and works across every tool in your stack simultaneously.
Here are 4 Claude Skills I demonstrated live:
- CV Ranker: Upload 5 CVs and a job spec. Scored and ranked in 20 seconds.

- LinkedIn Content Writer: Knows your voice, your pillars, your formatting. 5 posts in 10 minutes.
- Lead List Builder: Pulls companies matching your ICP with verified decision-maker contacts.
Once a Skill is built, anyone on your team can run it. Same quality, same speed, every time.
