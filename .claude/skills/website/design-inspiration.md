# Design Inspiration — for RecruiterGTM site + Pulse Recruit

A running reference of site designs worth borrowing *systems* from (layout, colour logic, components) — not branding. Take the pattern, never the identity: don't clone a competitor's exact look, logo, or copy. Reyhan flagged the first entry (hikmahaiagency.com) as a design he likes.

---

## 1. Hikmah AI Agency — hikmahaiagency.com (captured 2026-08-13)

Dubai AI-marketing agency. Weak on SEO (DR 3.5, 0 Google traffic) but the **design is genuinely strong** — a data-driven, credible SaaS aesthetic. Worth modelling for **Pulse Recruit's marketing site** especially, and usable for RecruiterGTM.

### What makes it work
- **Data-driven, minimal-ornamentation SaaS look** — reads as a serious product, not a coach's landing page. Credibility over decoration.
- **An interactive centrepiece, not a static hero.** The hero is a clickable funnel visualisation you can explore, not just a headline + image. Gives an immediate "this is a system" feeling — perfect for how RecruiterGTM/Pulse sell *systems*.
- **Contextual colour coding as information**, not decoration (see palette).
- **Proof density** — client metrics, ROAS numbers, founder bios, a 20-item portfolio gallery. Every section earns trust.

### Layout & structure (the reusable skeleton)
1. **Hero:** bold cost/outcome headline + subhead + two CTAs ("Get Started" / "Book a free call"). Interactive funnel viz as the visual.
2. **Interactive funnel visualisation** (centrepiece) — the 5 stages clickable, each expanding to a live example.
3. **Five-stage breakdown** with live examples per stage.
4. **Case studies** with anonymised client metrics.
5. **Portfolio gallery** — horizontal-scroll, "20 live concepts, zero templates".
6. **Founder bios** (credibility).
7. **Contact form.**

### Colour system
- **Base:** deep navy / charcoal backgrounds (dark-first).
- **Contextual accents used as meaning:** orange = paid, green = organic, red = budget leaks. Colour carries information in the funnel diagram, not just style.
- Dark/light contrast for readability; generous whitespace throughout.

### Typography
- Clean modern sans-serif; varied weights for hierarchy; large readable headlines.

### How to apply it to our sites
- **Pulse Recruit:** the interactive-funnel-viz pattern maps perfectly to showing the ATS/recruit workflow (source → screen → place). Dark base + contextual accent colours to distinguish pipeline stages.
- **RecruiterGTM:** borrow the "interactive system diagram as hero" for the 5-pillar framework — make the 5 pillars a clickable diagram, each expanding to what it does + proof. Keep our own brand colours (don't adopt Hikmah's navy/orange verbatim); take the *structure* — interactive hero, contextual colour, proof density, horizontal portfolio/case-study gallery.
- **Guardrail:** take the layout system and interaction ideas, not their exact palette/branding/copy. Our identity stays ours.

### What NOT to copy
- Their SEO approach (near-zero Google, low-quality 414-refdomain link profile) — design only.
