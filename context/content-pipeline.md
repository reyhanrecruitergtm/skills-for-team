### Wednesday 2026-03-26 — Claude Dispatch (AI Opinion)

**Status:** Draft — pending Reyhan approval
**Topic:** Claude Dispatch by Anthropic — from chatbot to delegating tasks from your phone
**CTA:** Comment PLAYBOOK
**Research file:** `research/2026-03-21-claude-dispatch-what-it-is-how-to-use-it-use-cases-for-recru.md`

**Draft:**

Most people still think of AI as something you talk to.

Anthropic just made it something you delegate to.

Claude Dispatch launched on March 17. You send a task from your phone, and Claude executes it on your desktop while you are nowhere near your desk.

Not a chat. Not a prompt. A worker.

Here is what that looks like in practice.

I sent a message from my phone: "Research these 50 companies, score them against my ICP, and leave me a ranked list."

By the time I sat down, it was done.

That used to take 2-3 hours of manual work.

The honest breakdown:

✅ All data stays local — nothing leaves your machine
✅ Near 100% success rate on research and analysis tasks
✅ Connects to Gmail, Slack, Notion, your CRM
✅ Can run on a schedule — daily, weekly, every Monday morning

❌ Around 50% success on complex multi-app automation
❌ Desktop has to stay awake while it runs
❌ Requires Claude Max ($100-200/month)

The shift is not really about the tool.

It is about the mental model.

You stop asking AI "what should I do?" and start telling it "go do this while I'm busy."

That is not a chatbot. That is an operator who works while you sleep.

For a recruitment business, the use cases are obvious:

→ Score a TAM of 200 companies against your ICP before your morning coffee
→ Draft personalised outreach angles while you are on a client call
→ Pull a full pipeline brief every Monday before you open your inbox

I am building this into how I run OutboundOS setups for clients.

The bottleneck was never the strategy. It was execution bandwidth.

Dispatch removes the bandwidth problem.
