## Clay

**Pricing change announced: 2026-03-11**
- Announced via official blog post: "The thinking behind our new pricing: our internal memo" by Karan Parekh
- Also communicated via email to existing customers
- Existing customers get 30 days to choose which legacy plan to keep; legacy options close to new signups after that window

**Old pricing (what clients were on):**
- Explorer: $350/mo — 10,000 credits (unified — enrichment + AI + actions all from same pool)

**New pricing (as of 2026-03-11):**
| Plan | Price | Data Credits | Actions |
|------|-------|-------------|---------|
| Launch | $350/mo | 6,000 | 15,000 |
| Growth | $495/mo | 6,000 | 40,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom |

Note: Data credits and actions are now separated. Data credits = external enrichment lookups. Actions = running rows, AI steps, automations.

**Impact on our clients:**
- Old Explorer ($350) gave 10,000 unified credits → processed 2,500–3,000 leads/mo at 4 credits/row
- New Launch ($350) gives only 6,000 data credits → processes ~1,500 leads/mo at 4 credits/row
- To hit old volume (2,500–3,000 leads): need $560–670/mo total (base + top-ups at ~$0.053/credit)
- Using own OpenAI + Prospeo API reduces data credit cost per row, but floor is still ~3 credits/row for a quality playbook

**Our position:**
- At ~$600/mo, Clay alone still requires Lemlist + copywriting + an operator
- Our full GTM Engine (Clay + Lemlist + Pin.com + Claude) hits the same price point as a complete system
- This pricing change is a natural conversation starter for upgrading clients to the full GTM Engine
