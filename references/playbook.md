## Pricing Learnings

- Standard 3-month pilot rate: €2,500/mo. Use "exclusive pricing" framing for warm leads (€2,000/mo)
- 6-month retainer commitment unlocks lower rate (€1,800/mo) — use as a closing lever, not a default offer
- Never lead with the discount. Anchor high, then offer exclusive rate with a reason (past relationship, early adopter, proof of concept)
- Duncan negotiation (Apr 2026): anchored at €2,500 standard, offered €2,000 exclusive, €1,800 for 6-month lock-in. His choice, not our concession

## Sales Patterns

- Proposals that close: Tella video walkthrough + HTML proposal + "when do you want to start" CTA
- Proposals that stall: "open in your own time" CTA, no video, no urgency
- Discovery calls: always analyse Fireflies notes before building any proposal — never assume single-service
- The "I have this guy" pitch works: lead with specific value ("we've built engines for 200+ recruitment niches") not features ("we offer outbound services")

## Hiring Learnings

- Attitude > aptitude (Caan). Screen for mindset first, train skills second
- The high-agency gap is real: team operates as task-takers, not domain owners. Goal is each person proactively manages their area
- Robyn's sourcing gap (Q1 2026): may not be a volume problem — may be a filtering problem. Apply Caan's attitude filter

## Content Learnings

- LinkedIn posts that work: hook on its own line, one sentence per line, real numbers, real client names, story-driven
- Posts that don't work: salesy, hashtag-heavy, generic CTAs, big blocks of text
- Content pillars (to be defined using Gray's 3x3x3): Systems / Talent / GTM
- Target: 5x LinkedIn/week. Currently a time drain — needs batching system

## Client Delivery Learnings

- Reply handling and meeting booking always stays with the client. No exceptions. This is the line
- Build Phase (months 1-3) is a fixed commitment. No early termination
- Everything we build lives inside the client's own tools and accounts. They own it
- Our frameworks and methodology stay ours. The engine built for them is theirs
- Weekly reporting call + Slack channel + end of month review = the standard communication stack
- 3-month pilot proves the concept. 6-month extension only if consistently delivering conversations
