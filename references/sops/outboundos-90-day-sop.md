# OutboundOS — 90-Day Client Delivery SOP

_Standard checklist for every OutboundOS retainer client. Each week maps to tasks in the client's Notion record and deliverables in their shared Google Doc._

**Two tracking systems:**
- **Notion (internal):** Task tracking for the team. Client does NOT have access.
- **Client Google Doc (shared):** Living document in the client's project folder. Client has view/comment access. Updated weekly with progress, metrics, and deliverables.

---

## MONTH 1: BUILD & LAUNCH

### Week 1 — Kickoff & Onboarding
- [ ] Kickoff call with client (60 min) — define ICP, niche, positioning, target geography
- [ ] Collect tool access: Clay, Lemlist, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, CRM credentials
- [ ] Send intake form — confirm ICP details, ideal client profile, deal size, service lines
- [ ] Set up dedicated Slack channel (client + RecruiterGTM team)
- [ ] Schedule recurring weekly reporting call (30 min, same day/time each week)
- [ ] Begin email domain warmup (2-3 sending domains, 2 mailboxes per domain)
- [ ] Create client Google Doc in project folder — share with client (view/comment access)
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: kickoff notes, confirmed ICP, agreed timeline

### Week 2 — Market Mapping & TAM Build
- [ ] Build TAM in Clay — ICP-based, segmented by company size, hiring volume, industry
- [ ] Enrich TAM: verified emails, LinkedIn profile URLs, company data, intent signals
- [ ] Identify 4 intent signal playbooks: hiring surge, funding/growth, leadership change, competitor churn
- [ ] TAM count documented: total companies, total contacts, contacts per segment
- [ ] Client reviews and approves TAM (via Slack or call)
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: TAM summary, segment breakdown, enrichment stats

### Week 3 — Copy & Playbook Build
- [ ] Write Email outreach copy for all 4 playbooks (3-touch sequence per playbook)
- [ ] Write LinkedIn connection request + 2-step follow-up sequence for all 4 playbooks
- [ ] Map personalisation variables per playbook: company name, trigger event, role context
- [ ] Draft A/B subject lines (2 variants per playbook) and A/B hooks (2 variants per playbook)
- [ ] Build all Email sequences in Lemlist (4 playbooks x 3 touches = 12 emails)
- [ ] Build all LinkedIn sequences in Lemlist (4 playbooks x 3 touches = 12 steps)
- [ ] Client reviews and approves all copy (email + LinkedIn)
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: all copy drafts, playbook descriptions, A/B test plan

### Week 4 — Launch Prep & Go Live
- [ ] Final deliverability check: domain health score, warmup completion, inbox placement test
- [ ] Configure Lemlist campaigns: daily send limits, throttling rules, sending windows
- [ ] Configure LinkedIn automation: connection request cadence, daily limits, follow-up timing
- [ ] Set up CRM integration: positive replies auto-tagged, stage logic mapped (Interested → Call Booked → Proposal Sent → Closed)
- [ ] GO LIVE — first Email + LinkedIn campaigns sent
- [ ] Day 1 send volume: 50 emails/day + 30 LinkedIn connection requests/day (ramp target)
- [ ] Week 4 report delivered to client: launch confirmation, send volumes, initial open/reply rates
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: launch date, campaign structure, initial metrics

---

## MONTH 2: OPTIMISE & TEST

### Week 5 — Monitor & First Data
- [ ] Monitor Email deliverability daily: open rates (target >50%), bounce rates (<3%), spam complaints (<0.1%)
- [ ] Monitor LinkedIn: connection acceptance rate (target >25%), reply rate
- [ ] Track and categorise all replies: positive, neutral, objection, not interested
- [ ] Positive replies routed to client CRM with auto-tag
- [ ] Weekly report: Email delivery metrics + LinkedIn metrics + reply breakdown
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: Week 5 metrics table

### Week 6 — First Optimisation Pass
- [ ] Analyse A/B test results: winning subject lines, hooks, CTAs identified
- [ ] Kill underperforming Email variants, scale winning variants
- [ ] Kill underperforming LinkedIn sequences, scale winners
- [ ] Refresh TAM segment — activate next 1/3 of TAM (new contacts into sequences)
- [ ] Adjust personalisation if reply data shows specific angles resonating
- [ ] Weekly report: optimisation changes made + before/after metrics
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: A/B results, changes made, updated metrics

### Week 7 — Expand & Iterate
- [ ] Launch second wave Email campaigns with refined copy
- [ ] Launch second wave LinkedIn sequences with refined messaging
- [ ] Test 1-2 new angles or playbooks based on reply patterns
- [ ] LinkedIn sequence adjustments: timing, follow-up intervals based on acceptance/reply rates
- [ ] Weekly report: Week 7 vs Week 5-6 comparison
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: new angles tested, comparison metrics

### Week 8 — Month 2 Review
- [ ] End of Month 2 performance review call with client (30-60 min)
- [ ] Full Month 2 report: total conversations generated, Email reply rate, LinkedIn reply rate, pipeline impact
- [ ] Agree optimisation priorities for Month 3
- [ ] Data refresh: new prospects added to TAM, stale contacts removed, updated intent signals
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: Month 2 summary, agreed Month 3 priorities

---

## MONTH 3: SCALE & VALIDATE

### Week 9 — Scale Winning Playbooks
- [ ] Increase daily send volume on highest-performing Email playbooks
- [ ] Increase LinkedIn outreach on highest-performing sequences
- [ ] Expand TAM: activate final 1/3 of prospects
- [ ] Weekly report: scaling metrics, volume increase vs reply rate impact
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: Week 9 metrics, scaling decisions

### Week 10 — Advanced Testing
- [ ] Test new messaging angles on fresh segments (Email + LinkedIn)
- [ ] Experiment with timing changes: send days, send hours
- [ ] Experiment with cadence changes: shorter/longer follow-up gaps
- [ ] Track conversation-to-meeting conversion rate
- [ ] Weekly report: test results, conversion data
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: Week 10 test results, conversion metrics

### Week 11 — Final Push & Pipeline Cleanup
- [ ] Full pipeline review with client: all conversations, stages, next steps
- [ ] Ensure CRM data is clean: stages updated, deal owners assigned, no orphan leads
- [ ] Final A/B tests on remaining variables
- [ ] Weekly report: Month 3 trajectory, projected final numbers
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: pipeline review, projected 90-day totals

### Week 12 — 90-Day Performance Review & Continuation Decision
- [ ] End of pilot performance review call with client (60 min)
- [ ] Full 90-day report delivered: total conversations, Email reply rate, LinkedIn reply rate, meetings booked, pipeline generated
- [ ] ROI analysis: cost vs conversations vs pipeline value
- [ ] Joint decision on continuation into GTM Engine Management Phase
- [ ] If continuing: agree managed service scope, next quarter priorities, any pricing adjustments
- [ ] Update client Google Doc: final 90-day report, continuation decision, next steps

---

## Benchmarks (per the agreement)
- **Target:** 5-10 DM conversations per month from cold outbound
- **Email open rate:** >50%
- **Email reply rate:** >2-5%
- **LinkedIn connection acceptance:** >25%
- **Bounce rate:** <3%
- **Spam complaints:** <0.1%

## Notes
- Weekly reports are delivered every Friday via Slack + updated in client Google Doc
- All campaign data, TAM exports, and copy documents live in the client's project folder
- Notion tasks are internal only — client never sees Notion
- Client Google Doc is the single source of truth the client sees
